Is global pixel accuracy misleading?
yes, global pixel accuracy can hide poor performance on rare classes because background pixels dominate the image.

Why are small classes often harder to evaluate?
small classes contain fewer pixels, so each mistake has a larger impact on their accuracy and they are easier to miss or confuse at boundaries.

CLASSIFICATION VS SEGMENTATION
==============================

What does classification output?
classification outputs one label for the entire image, for example river, forest, or residential area.

What does segmentation output?
segmentation outputs a class label for every pixel in the image, producing a full segmentation mask.

Which task is more suitable for land-cover mapping?
semantic segmentation is more suitable because it shows where each land-cover class is located across the scene.

Which task is more suitable for quick scene categorization?
image classification is more suitable because it gives a fast overall scene label.

Why is segmentation more computationally demanding?
segmentation must predict a label for each pixel instead of one label per image.

How could segmentation support mission operations?
segmentation can support mission operations by detecting water bodies, urban areas, vegetation, etc. at pixel level, helping with decision making and planning.

SEGMENTATION ANALYSIS
=====================

Pixel accuracy: 0.9949

Visual observations:
segmentation performed really well in general.
the problem arised when it comes to predict the right figures/borders of classes - squares were pretty rounded and all the borders were slightly deformed.

Most accurate class:
the water class

Most difficult class:
the urban class

Boundary errors:
the boundaries weren't as perfectly sharp

Small object errors:
small objects were the most deformed ones

Engineering conclusions:
the model isn't perfect but it does its job instead of outputing a completely random result.

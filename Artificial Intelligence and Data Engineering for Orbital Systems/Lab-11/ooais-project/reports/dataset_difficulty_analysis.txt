DATASET DIFFICULTY ANALYSIS
============================

Changes made:
- Thinner water regions (width reduced from 15 to 6 pixels)
- Smaller urban blocks (size reduced from 12x12 to 6x6 pixels)
- More RGB noise (range increased from +-15 to +-30)

Original pixel accuracy: 0.9949
New pixel accuracy: 0.9965

Did pixel accuracy decrease?
no

Which class became harder?
urban became clearly harder.

Did small structures become more difficult?
yes, smaller urban blocks and thinner water strips were harder to segment accurately.

What does this suggest about real Earth Observation data?
real earth observation data is much harder than those simple made up scenes because it contains irregular shapes, less perfect borders, and overall it is much more complex.

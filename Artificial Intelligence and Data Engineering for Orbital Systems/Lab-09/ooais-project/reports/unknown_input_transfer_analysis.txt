UNKNOWN INPUT TRANSFER ANALYSIS
================================

1. Does the transfer model force a known class prediction?
yes

2. Does it give high confidence on unknown input?
often, yes

3. Is transfer learning enough for OOD detection?
no

4. What additional mechanism is needed?
uncertainty estimation (ensembles/MC dropout).

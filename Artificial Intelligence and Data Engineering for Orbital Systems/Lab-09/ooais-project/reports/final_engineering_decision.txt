FINAL ENGINEERING DECISION
=========================

Selected model:
ResNet18 with fine-tuning

Technical justification:
ResNet18 fine-tuned achieved 0.9889 test accuracy, outperforming the frozen-transfer and SimpleCNN baselines
fine-tuning adapts high-level features while keeping pretrained low-level representations

Expected advantages:
highest accuracy and clearer class separation

Possible risks:
larger model increases inference cost and can overfit

Deployment considerations:
make model probabilities reliable and detect unfamiliar/odd images
monitor predictions and have humans check low-confidence cases
